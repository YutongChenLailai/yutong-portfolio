Research Focus / 研究方向

I am an HCI researcher and creative technologist studying how interactive and generative systems shape cognition, judgment, agency, and creative practice. My current work focuses on feedback-driven human–AI co-creation: how generative systems enter ideation, iteration, decision-making, and creative exploration. Rather than treating AI primarily as a tool for efficiency or finished outputs, I study how sustained interaction changes the ways people think, explore possibilities, make creative decisions, and experience control over their work. I design and build interactive research prototypes, then use them in user studies to examine cognitive, behavioural, and creative processes in practice.

我是 HCI 研究者和创意技术实践者，研究交互与生成式系统如何影响人的认知、判断、能动性与创造实践。我目前关注基于反馈循环的人机共创，以及生成式系统如何介入构思、迭代、决策与创意探索。相比把 AI 主要视为提高效率或生成成品的工具，我更关心持续的人机互动如何改变人的思考方式、探索范围、创作决策，以及对作品的掌控感。方法上，我设计并搭建交互式研究原型，再通过用户研究考察真实互动中的认知、行为与创造过程。
