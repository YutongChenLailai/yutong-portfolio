About / 关于

I create experiences that sit between critical inquiry and sensory play.

我的创作位于批判性研究与感官体验之间，关注技术如何塑造感知、文化记忆与日常行为。

Yutong Chen is an interaction designer and creative technologist working across critical VR, moving image, installation and experimental interfaces.

陈宇同是一名交互设计师与创意技术实践者，创作媒介涵盖批判性虚拟现实、动态影像、互动装置与实验界面。

Her practice turns abstract systems—value, scarcity, heritage and social behaviour—into situations that can be entered, felt and questioned.

她把价值、稀缺性、文化遗产与社会行为等抽象系统，转化为可以进入、感受并提出质疑的体验。

She studied Digital Media Art at Harbin Institute of Technology, developing a foundation in moving image, 3D production and interactive media. She is now studying Information Experience Design at the Royal College of Art.

她本科就读于哈尔滨工业大学数字媒体艺术专业，建立了影像、三维制作与交互媒体的实践基础；现就读于英国皇家艺术学院 Information Experience Design，以批判性研究和具身交互继续拓展创作。
